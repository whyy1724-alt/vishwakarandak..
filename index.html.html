One fest. Every department. One champion.

Vishwakarandak is the annual flagship inter-departmental fest of VIT Pune. Departments battle across three pillars: Cultural, Sports and Tech. Every event adds points to the department’s tally, and the house with the most at the end takes the trophy.

It runs on house unity and departmental pride: classmates become teammates, seniors coach juniors, and a whole department turns up to cheer one performance.
